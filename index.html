GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What is FinPilot?

FinPilot is a personal finance management concept for tracking transactions, budgets and savings goals.

Does it connect to my bank account?

No. This portfolio version uses sample data and browser-based interactions. It does not connect to real bank accounts.

Will my demo transactions be saved?

The demo dashboard is designed to save supported changes in your browser using localStorage. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Is FinPilot financial advice?

No. The dashboard offers basic calculations from recorded data, not professional financial, investment or tax advice.
